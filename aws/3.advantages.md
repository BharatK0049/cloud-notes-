# Benefits of Cloud Computing (AWS)

AWS offers several advantages over traditional on-premises infrastructure.

---

## 1. Pay-as-you-go Pricing
### What it means
- No upfront hardware or infrastructure cost
- Pay only for the resources you actually use

### Why it matters
- Lower initial investment
- Easy to start small and scale gradually
- Costs stop when resources are deleted

---

## 2. Economies of Scale
### What it means
- AWS buys hardware in massive volumes
- Cost savings are passed on to customers

### Why it matters
- Cheaper compute, storage, and networking
- More cost-efficient than running private data centers

---

## 3. No Capacity Guessing
### What it means
- Resources can scale up or down automatically
- No need to predict future demand

### Why it matters
- Avoid over-provisioning (wasted money)
- Avoid under-provisioning (poor performance)
- Scaling happens in minutes, not months

---

## 4. Increased Speed and Agility
### What it means
- Quickly create, test, and delete environments
- Rapid experimentation

### Why it matters
- Faster development cycles
- Encourages innovation
- Fail fast without long-term cost

---

## 5. No Data Center Maintenance
### What it means
- AWS manages servers, power, cooling, and hardware failures

### Why it matters
- Teams focus on building products, not infrastructure
- Reduced operational overhead

---

## 6. Go Global in Minutes
### What it means
- Deploy applications in AWS Regions worldwide

### Why it matters
- Serve users closer to their location
- Global expansion without owning global infrastructure
